Title: Internal Memo: East region price revision
Document type: internal_memo
Product: iPhone 15 (IPHONE15)
Region: East
Date: 2026-04-28
Author: Commercial Finance, East

Following the new state levy on imported electronics, the East region will apply a 6% price increase to iPhone 15 from 1 May. Standard discount levels are unchanged.

Commercial expects some price-sensitive customers to defer purchases; a single-digit decline in unit volume is acceptable given the margin protection.
