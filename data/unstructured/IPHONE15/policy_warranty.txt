Title: Warranty Policy: iPhone 15
Document type: policy
Product: iPhone 15 (IPHONE15)
Region: ALL
Date: 2025-09-01
Author: Legal & Customer Experience

iPhone 15 is covered by a one-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects from the date of purchase.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, unauthorised repairs or normal wear. Extended cover is available through the protection plan.

Warranty claims are handled in store or through the support line and require proof of purchase.
