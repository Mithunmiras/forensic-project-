Title: Quality Control Report: iPhone 15 West region batches
Document type: quality_report
Product: iPhone 15 (IPHONE15)
Region: West
Date: 2026-04-06
Author: Quality Engineering

Batch QC flagged an elevated screen defect rate of 2.1% against a 0.3% baseline in units shipped to West region stores in early April (batches W-2604-11 to W-2604-19).

Most failures are display flicker and dead-pixel clusters appearing within the first week of use. Field returns citing a faulty screen have risen sharply at West region stores.

Recommended action: hold the affected batches pending root-cause analysis with the display panel vendor and prioritise replacement units for affected customers.
