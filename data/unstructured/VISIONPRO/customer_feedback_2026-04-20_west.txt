Title: Customer Feedback Summary: Vision Pro, West region
Document type: customer_feedback
Product: Vision Pro (VISIONPRO)
Region: West
Date: 2026-04-20
Author: Customer Support Analytics

Customer feedback in the West region during April is dominated by complaints about display calibration faults: users report colour drift in one eye after a few days of use.

Store technicians confirm the defect on returned units, and the QC team has opened an investigation into the affected batch.

Overall satisfaction for Vision Pro in the West fell from 4.5 to 3.6 out of 5.
