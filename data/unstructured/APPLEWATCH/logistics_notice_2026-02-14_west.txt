Title: Logistics Notice: WH_WEST_01 flood closure
Document type: logistics_notice
Product: Apple Watch (APPLEWATCH)
Region: West
Date: 2026-02-14
Author: Distribution Operations, West

Heavy rain flooded the loading bays at WH_WEST_01 on 14 February. The warehouse is closed while the site is cleaned and inspected, and inbound transport has been redirected.

Apple Watch stock held on the ground floor was damaged and written off; remaining stock cannot be dispatched until the warehouse reopens, expected in about six days.
