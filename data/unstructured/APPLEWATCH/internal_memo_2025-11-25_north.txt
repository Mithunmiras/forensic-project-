Title: Internal Memo: North region holiday campaign
Document type: internal_memo
Product: Apple Watch (APPLEWATCH)
Region: North
Date: 2025-11-25
Author: Marketing, North region

Marketing will run a four-week holiday gifting campaign for Apple Watch across North region TV, outdoor and social channels from 1 December.

The campaign has no price component: list prices and the standard discount stay unchanged. Store teams should plan for a demand surge and keep display stock replenished.

Additional inventory has been pre-positioned at WH_NORTH_01 so that the campaign does not cause stock-outs.
