Title: Market Intelligence Brief: Tablets, East region

Product: iPad Pro (IPADPRO)
Region: East

Competitor A launched its new flagship tablet in the East region on 5 January, bundled with a free keyboard and a carrier data plan at major electronics chains.

Store walk-throughs and partner feedback indicate that customers in the premium tablet segment are switching to the rival bundle; sales associates report more comparison shopping than usual.

No comparable launch has happened in North, South or West yet. Expect pressure on iPad Pro volumes in the East while the rival bundle is available.
