Title: Returns Policy: MacBook Air
Document type: policy
Product: MacBook Air (MACBOOKAIR)
Region: ALL
Date: 2025-09-01
Author: Legal & Customer Experience

Unopened MacBook Air units may be returned within the standard 14-day return window for a refund to the original payment method.

Opened units may be returned within the same period if they are complete and undamaged. A restocking fee does not apply.

This policy applies uniformly to all regions unless a regional policy update states otherwise.
