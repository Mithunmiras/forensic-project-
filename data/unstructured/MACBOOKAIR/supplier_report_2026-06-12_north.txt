Title: Supplier Advisory: MacBook Air display panels
Document type: supplier_report
Product: MacBook Air (MACBOOKAIR)
Region: North
Date: 2026-06-12
Author: Quanta Computer - Account Management

We are flagging a potential shortage of display panels for MacBook Air in July, following reduced output at a panel sub-supplier.

Current shipments to the North region distribution centre are unaffected; the risk applies to orders placed after 1 July. A shipment delay of up to two weeks is possible.

We will confirm by the end of June whether the delay will materialise.
