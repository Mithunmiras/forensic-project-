Title: Policy Update: West region extended return window
Document type: policy_update
Product: MacBook Air (MACBOOKAIR)
Region: West
Date: 2026-01-02
Author: Customer Experience, West

Effective 1 January, the return window for MacBook Air purchased in West region stores is extended from 14 to 30 days as a pilot of the new customer-satisfaction programme.

Customers may return opened units for a full refund within the extended window, no questions asked. Store teams should expect a higher volume of returns, most of them in resaleable condition.

The pilot will be reviewed at the end of the quarter.
