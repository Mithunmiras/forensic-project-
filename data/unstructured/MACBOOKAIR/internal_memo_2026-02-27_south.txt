Title: Internal Memo: South region pre-refresh clearance
Document type: internal_memo
Product: MacBook Air (MACBOOKAIR)
Region: South
Date: 2026-02-27
Author: Regional Manager, South

The South regional manager has approved a 15% promotional discount on MacBook Air for the whole of March to clear pre-refresh inventory ahead of the next model introduction.

Finance notes that the deeper markdown should lift unit volume substantially, but most of the gain will be offset by the lower realised price, so revenue should rise only slightly. Margin per unit will fall.

The promotion applies to South region retail and online channels only.
