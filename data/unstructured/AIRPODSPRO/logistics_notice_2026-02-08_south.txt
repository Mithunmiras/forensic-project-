Title: Logistics Notice: South region transport strike
Document type: logistics_notice
Product: AirPods Pro (AIRPODSPRO)
Region: South
Date: 2026-02-08
Author: Distribution Operations, South

A regional trucking strike began on 8 February, halting road transport from the port terminal to WH_SOUTH_01. Carrier partners have suspended all scheduled deliveries into the South region.

Warehouse stock of AirPods Pro is expected to run out within a day. Inbound pallets are held at the terminal until the strike is resolved.

Union talks are ongoing; carriers estimate 10 to 12 days before normal delivery schedules resume.
